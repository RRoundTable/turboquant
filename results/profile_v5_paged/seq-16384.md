## Analysis

**Dequant is NOT the bottleneck.** Combined K+V dequant = 316 cycles = **10%**.
HYP-032's warp-shuffle LUT already did its job; there's little left to win
on the dequant phase.

**WMMA QK is 41% of per-tile time.** At bdy=4:
- Q matrix fills rows 0..3 of the 16×16 MMA tile; rows 4..15 are zero-padded.
- Useful work = 4 queries × 16 KV tokens × head_dim FMAs per k-tile
- WMMA does 16 × 16 × 16 FMAs regardless of fill
- **Effective utilization: 4/16 = 25%** on the M dimension.
- 75% of WMMA throughput is wasted on zero-padded rows.

**V accumulate is 17% and uses scalar FMA** (not tensor cores). Loads scores
from smem and does FMA per-dim. Another lever but smaller.

**Softmax is 15%.** Online softmax rescale across tile_n=16 tokens. Could be
cut via warp-level reduction primitives but mid-size lever.

## Priority-ranked HYP-036 candidates

### A. Fix WMMA M-dim underutilization (biggest lever, ~28% total)
Two paths:
1. Use `mma.m16n8k16` instead of `mma.m16n16k16`. Same throughput per SM cycle
   but only 8 KV tokens per tile, doubling the tile count. Trades off the N
   dimension instead of M. Marginal if Q still fills only 4/16 rows.
2. **Better**: pack multiple (batch, kv_head) into the M dimension. E.g.,
   4 kv_heads × 4 queries per head = 16 rows, M fully utilized. Requires
   restructuring the block grid — one block handles 4 kv_heads instead of 1.
   Expected: cut WMMA_QK from 924 ns to ~230 ns, **save ~30% of total**.

### B. Promote V accumulate to WMMA (mid lever, ~8% total)
V accumulate is `O += P · V` where P is [bdy, tile_n] attn weights and V is
[tile_n, head_dim]. Currently scalar FMA per dim. As WMMA:
- A = P: [16, 16] fp16 (rows 0..bdy-1 valid)
- B = V^T: [16, head_dim/16] tiles
- Same bdy=4 M-dim underutilization as QK
- Combined with (A): much bigger win.
Expected standalone: cut V_accumulate from 370 ns to ~150 ns, save ~10%.

### C. Faster softmax (small lever, ~5% total)
Warp-wide max/sum reduction via `__shfl_xor_sync`. Modest.

## Plan

Open HYP-036 against lever A (+B). Lever A is the *shape-changing* fix;
bdy=4 packing is the right call because it naturally brings B along —
once M is fully utilized for QK, extending the same pattern to V is a
small incremental change to the same kernel.

Expected total improvement at seq=4096 (chunk=128):
- Current: 64 μs
- After WMMA re-packing: ~45 μs (saves 30% of kernel time)
- After V-WMMA: ~40 μs
- Puts us within 1.0× of FlashInfer at seq=1024–2048, within 1.0× at 4096.
